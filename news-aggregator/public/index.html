Our Premise

  1. AI analyzes multiple sources across the political spectrum.
  2. Presents the facts neutrally, with full context.
  3. Shows the lean of every news organization covering it — left, center, or right.

Coverage of the same event often reads like coverage of two different events, depending on which outlet you open. ApageI rewrites each story from scratch, using only facts corroborated across multiple outlets, to strip out loaded language and framing from any direction — then shows you exactly who covered it and how far left or right that outlet tends to lean, so the framing is visible instead of invisible.

The goal isn’t an opinion-free internet. It’s knowing which parts of what you read are the event, and which parts are the editor.
